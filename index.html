Getting Started

Everything you need to know to get started with FlowDeck.

How to invite teammates to a workspace

Go to Settings → Team and click Invite Member. Enter your teammate's email address and select their role — Admin, Editor, or Viewer. They'll receive an invite link by email, valid for 48 hours. Admins can resend invites from the same settings page.

How to archive a completed project board

Open the board, click the ⋯ menu in the top-right corner, and select Archive Board. Archived boards are hidden from your main view but remain fully searchable. You can restore them at any time from Settings → Archived Boards.

Forgot username — how to recover account access

On the FlowDeck login page, click Forgot username? and enter the email address associated with your account. You'll receive an email with your username within a few minutes. If you no longer have access to that email address, contact support@flowdeck.io with proof of account ownership.

How to create your first workspace

After signing up, click + Create Workspace on the home screen. Enter a workspace name, choose an icon and color, then click Create. You become the workspace owner by default and can invite teammates immediately from the welcome banner.

How to create a new board from a template

Click + Add Board in the left sidebar, then select From Template. Browse categories such as Project Management, CRM, or Marketing, preview a template, and click Use Template. The board opens with sample columns and groups you can rename to fit your workflow.

How to add columns to a board

On any board, click the + button at the far right of the column header row. Choose a column type (Status, Person, Date, Text, Numbers, and more), give it a name, and click Add. You can reorder columns by dragging their headers.

How to create and organize groups on a board

Click + Add Group at the bottom of the board (or use the board menu). Name the group and optionally pick a color. Drag items between groups to reorganize work. Collapse a group by clicking its chevron to keep long boards scannable.

How to add an item (row) to a board

Click + Add Item at the bottom of any group, type a name, and press Enter. You can also use Ctrl/Cmd + Enter to create another item immediately. Fill in columns afterward, or use the item card panel for a fuller edit view.

How to create subitems under a parent item

Hover an item and click the subitem icon (or open the item and choose Add Subitem). Subitems inherit the parent board’s column layout and nest under the parent row. Collapse the parent to hide its subitems when you need a cleaner view.

How to assign people to an item

Click a Person column cell on the item, then select one or more teammates from the list. Only workspace members appear here — invite someone first if they’re missing. Assignees receive an in-app notification and optional email, depending on their notification settings.

How to set and change an item’s status

Click the Status cell on an item and pick a label (e.g. Working on it, Done, Stuck). Admins can customize labels and colors from the column settings menu. Status changes can trigger automations if any are configured on the board.

How to set due dates and deadlines

Click a Date column cell, pick a date from the calendar, and optionally set a time. Overdue dates appear in red. You can also set date ranges for milestones by enabling End date in the column settings.

How to leave updates and comments on an item

Open an item to open its side panel, then type in the Updates area. Use @mentions to notify teammates. Attach files with the paperclip icon. Updates stay on the item timeline so the full discussion history is searchable later.

How to attach files to an item

Open the item panel and click Add file, or use a Files column on the board. Drag and drop uploads up to 100 MB per file on Pro and Enterprise plans. Supported types include images, PDFs, Office docs, and zip archives.

How to duplicate a board

Open the board, click the ⋯ menu, and choose Duplicate Board. Choose whether to copy structure only or structure plus items. The duplicate appears in the same workspace with “ (copy)” appended to the name — rename it anytime from the board header.

How to move a board to another workspace

Open the board ⋯ menu and select Move to Workspace. Pick a destination workspace where you have Admin access, then confirm. Board members who aren’t in the destination workspace lose access unless you invite them there afterward.

How to create a custom board view (Table, Kanban, Timeline)

Above the board, click + Add View and choose Table, Kanban, Timeline, Calendar, or Chart. Configure filters and visible columns, then name and save the view. Switch views anytime from the view tabs without changing the underlying data.

How to filter and sort items on a board

Click Filter in the board toolbar, then add conditions (e.g. Status is Done, Person is me). Use Sort to order by any column ascending or descending. Save the combination as a new view if you want to reuse it later.

How to search across boards and workspaces

Press Ctrl/Cmd + K or click the search bar at the top of FlowDeck. Type a keyword, item name, or update snippet. Results include boards, items, docs, and people you can access. Use the filter chips to narrow by type (boards only, items only, etc.).

How to set up your profile and notification preferences

Click your avatar → My Profile to update your name, photo, and timezone. Open Notifications to choose email vs in-app alerts for assignments, mentions, and due dates. You can mute a specific board from that board’s notification bell.

How to switch between workspaces

Click the workspace name in the top-left corner to open the workspace switcher. Select another workspace you’re a member of, or use Browse Workspaces to find ones you’ve been invited to. Your last-opened boards are remembered per workspace.

How to pin favorite boards to the sidebar

Hover a board in the left sidebar and click the star icon to pin it under Favorites. Drag favorites to reorder them. Unpin anytime by clicking the star again — the board stays in its original folder.

How to create folders to organize boards

In the left sidebar, click + Add → New Folder, name it, and drag boards into it. Folders are workspace-scoped; nesting up to two levels is supported. Collapse folders you don’t use daily to keep the sidebar tidy.

How to share a board with guest users

Open Share on the board, enter the guest’s email, and choose Viewer or Commenter access. Guests only see that board (and boards you explicitly share), not the full workspace. Guest seats count toward your plan’s guest limit.

How to export a board to CSV or Excel

Open the board ⋯ menu → Export, then choose CSV or Excel. Exports include visible columns and groups; apply filters first if you only want a subset. Large boards may email you a download link when the export finishes.

How to restore a deleted item from the recycle bin

Go to Settings → Recycle Bin (or open the board menu → Recycle Bin). Find the item, select it, and click Restore. Items stay in the bin for 30 days on most plans; after that they are permanently removed.

How to customize status labels and colors

Click the Status column header → Settings → Edit labels. Rename labels, change colors, add new ones, or deactivate unused labels. Changes apply to the whole board; existing items keep their label until you update them.

How to use keyboard shortcuts in FlowDeck

Press ? anywhere in FlowDeck to open the shortcuts cheat sheet. Common ones: N new item, C open item card, Ctrl/Cmd + K search, Esc close panels. Shortcuts work on web; the mobile apps use long-press gestures instead.

How to set your default landing board

Open a board you use daily, click the ⋯ menu, and select Set as Home Board. The next time you log in, FlowDeck opens that board instead of the workspace overview. Clear it from the same menu if you prefer the overview again.

How to invite someone with a specific role (Admin, Editor, Viewer)

Go to Settings → Team → Invite Member, enter their email, and choose a role before sending. Admins manage members and settings; Editors create and edit boards; Viewers can read and comment only. You can change a member’s role later from the Team page.

How to copy an item to another board

Open the item’s ⋯ menu (or right-click the row) and choose Move to / Copy to Board. Select the destination board and group, then choose Copy to leave the original in place. Matching column types map automatically; unmatched fields are skipped.

How to batch-edit multiple items at once

Check the boxes next to the items you want to update (or use Shift-click for a range). Use the bulk-actions bar to change status, assignee, dates, or move items to another group. Confirm the change — it applies to all selected items immediately.

How to install and sign in on the FlowDeck mobile app

Download FlowDeck from the App Store or Google Play, open the app, and sign in with the same email you use on web (or SSO if your workspace requires it). Push notifications for assignments and mentions can be enabled during onboarding or later under Profile → Notifications.

How do I set up an automation trigger?

Open your board and click Automations in the top toolbar. Click + Add Automation, then choose a trigger (e.g., "When status changes to Done") and an action (e.g., "Send an email notification"). Review the preview and click Activate. Automations run immediately once active.

Duplicate email notifications after re-enabling automation

This is a known issue: when an automation is re-enabled, any items that already match the trigger condition fire once immediately. To avoid this, temporarily set affected items to a neutral status before re-enabling, then update them after the automation is live.

Slack integration double-posting on status change

Double-posting typically happens when both a Slack integration and an automation are configured for the same trigger. Check Integrations → Slack and Automations to confirm only one is active for that event. Remove or disable the duplicate, then test with a single status change.

Request to transfer account ownership to new admin

The current account owner must go to Settings → Account → Ownership Transfer, select the new owner from the member list, and confirm with their password. The new owner will receive a confirmation email. Note: ownership transfer is immediate and cannot be undone without the new owner's cooperation.

Mobile app keeps logging out on Android

This is a known issue affecting Android 13+ devices with aggressive battery optimization enabled. Go to your Android Settings → Apps → FlowDeck → Battery and set it to Unrestricted. If the issue persists, try clearing the app cache. A fix is included in the next mobile release (v4.2.1).

Annual renewal charge — why was I billed now?

FlowDeck subscriptions renew automatically on the same date each year. You can find your renewal date in Settings → Billing → Subscription. A reminder email is sent 14 days before renewal. If you were charged unexpectedly, check that your billing email matches your notification preferences.

Annual vs monthly billing — upgrade charge confusion

When you upgrade from monthly to annual mid-cycle, you're charged the annual rate immediately and your remaining monthly credit is applied as a prorated deduction. The exact amount credited is shown in the upgrade confirmation screen before you confirm.

Refund request within 14-day money-back window

FlowDeck offers a full refund within 14 days of your initial purchase or annual renewal. Email support@flowdeck.io with your account email and the charge date. Refunds are processed within 5–10 business days to your original payment method.
